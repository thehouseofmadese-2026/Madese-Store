How A Madese Piece Is Born

Not a factory line. A pipeline we built ourselves — and we'll happily show you the messy middle on Instagram.

Step 01 / Design

Sketched in CAD

Every piece starts as parametric geometry we model in-house. If it looks like everything else on Amazon, it gets deleted.

Step 02 / Slice

Sliced & tuned

Wall counts, infill, supports — tuned per product so it's strong where it matters and light where it doesn't.

Step 03 / Print

Grown layer by layer

Printed in PETG on our farm. Hours per piece. The layer lines aren't a flaw — they're the signature.

Step 04 / Finish

Hand-finished

Deburred, checked, and packed by an actual human in Ichalkaranji who will take it personally if it arrives ugly.
